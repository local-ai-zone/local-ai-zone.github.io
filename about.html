About the Project

Local AI Zone was created to demystify the world of local artificial intelligence. Our goal is to provide a curated, easy-to-navigate resource for discovering and running powerful AI models on your own personal computer. We believe in the power of private, offline, and accessible AI for everyone.

About the Creator

My name is Hussain Nazary, and I am a passionate software developer and the founder of this project. I specialize in creating user-friendly desktop applications that leverage the power of local AI.

My flagship project is the GGUF Loader, a popular open-source tool that makes it simple for anyone to run GGUF models on their computer. My work is driven by a commitment to open-source principles and the belief that powerful technology should be in the hands of the user.

I created Local AI Zone to be the central hub for my work and a resource for the entire community.
